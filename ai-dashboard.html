01 · The opportunity

Dashboards show data. Users need direction.

Traditional dashboards often place the responsibility on users to interpret multiple metrics, identify problems and decide what to do next. The opportunity was to rethink the experience around decisions and intent rather than simply presenting more information.

The existing experience

Users navigate between charts, tables, filters and reports to understand what is happening and determine their next move.

The design opportunity

Bring relevant insights, context and recommended actions together so users can move from understanding a situation to acting on it.

02 · Problem framing

What if the dashboard understood the user's next question?

01 — Too much information, not enough prioritisation.
02 — Users have to connect insights across different screens.
03 — Identifying the next best action takes time and effort.
04 — Insights and execution are separated across workflows.

Design hypothesis

What if the product could interpret user intent, surface the most relevant context, recommend the next best action and keep the user in control of important decisions?

03 · Experience strategy

A shift in the interaction model.

Instead of adding a chat window beside an existing dashboard, the experience should become fundamentally more intelligent. The system brings forward relevant work while users retain control over decisions.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Recognise intent

Interpret the user's question, goal or business context.

02 · ANTICIPATE

Surface insights

Prioritise important changes, opportunities and risks.

03 · ACT

Enable decisions

Recommend actions and request approval before consequential changes.

04 · Proposed experience

From passive metrics to proactive intelligence.

FEATURE 01

Intent-led workspace

A contextual starting point that helps users express what they want to achieve and see relevant information.

FEATURE 02

Prioritised insights

Business signals are translated into clear, relevant summaries instead of endless metric scanning.

FEATURE 03

Recommended actions

Suggestions explain why an action matters and what the user can do next.

FEATURE 04

Human-in-the-loop control

Users can review, edit, approve or dismiss recommendations before consequential actions are taken.

06 · Design principles

Intelligence should build trust, not remove control.

Clarity over complexity — Show the right information at the right moment.
Context over raw data — Explain what changed and why it matters.
Transparency over black-box AI — Make recommendations understandable.
Human control by design — Keep consequential actions reviewable and reversible where possible.

07 · Reflection

Better dashboards are not about showing more.

The central design challenge is not simply making analytics easier to read. It is helping people make better-informed decisions with less unnecessary effort. An intent-led experience can make complex information more approachable while preserving transparency and user control.

What I would validate next

Test whether users can identify priorities faster, understand why an insight matters, evaluate the quality of recommendations and feel confident before approving an action. These are proposed evaluation areas, not measured results.
