01 · Project overview

Relationships start with trust.

Online matrimony platforms help people discover potential partners, but creating a profile and interacting with strangers can involve uncertainty. This concept explores how verification, useful context and AI-assisted discovery could make the experience more reassuring and relevant.

The challenge

How might we help people feel more confident about the authenticity of profiles while making partner discovery easier?

The opportunity

Bring identity assurance and thoughtful personalisation into the core experience, without making onboarding feel intimidating.

02 · Problem framing

Discovery is only useful when users feel confident.

01 — Users may be uncertain about profile authenticity.
02 — Lengthy onboarding can create friction before users see value.
03 — Too many profiles can make discovery feel overwhelming.
04 — Users need context to understand why a profile may be relevant.

Design hypothesis

If verification is clear, onboarding is purposeful and recommendations are explainable, users may feel more confident exploring relevant connections.

03 · Trust and verification

Three layers of identity verification.

The onboarding concept offers three verification methods. Each one serves a distinct purpose and gives users a clear understanding of what they are being asked to verify.

01 · CONTACT

Contact verification

Confirm access to a contact method through a one-time verification code.

02 · GOVERNMENT ID

Identity document

Submit an eligible government identity document for verification.

03 · FACE

Face verification

Complete a guided face check to support confidence that the profile represents a real person.

Design consideration

Verification should communicate what is collected, why it is needed, how it is protected and what happens if a user cannot complete a step. A verification badge should not be presented as a guarantee of someone's intentions or character.

05 · AI-assisted discovery

Move from endless browsing to relevant discovery.

AI can help organise discovery around user preferences and profile context. The experience should make recommendations understandable and allow users to adjust their preferences.

FEATURE 01

Personalised suggestions

Prioritise profiles using preferences and signals users have chosen to share.

FEATURE 02

Contextual insights

Explain shared interests or preference alignment without making unsupported claims.

FEATURE 03

Smarter filters

Help users refine discovery with clear controls and transparent preferences.

FEATURE 04

User-controlled AI

Let users edit preferences, understand recommendations and control personalisation.

06 · Experience principles

Designing for confidence, dignity and choice.

Privacy by design — Explain data collection and give users control over visibility.
Inclusive onboarding — Make verification guidance clear and accessible.
Transparent recommendations — Explain why a profile is suggested.
Respectful discovery — Avoid reducing people to scores or opaque compatibility labels.
Human choice — Keep users in control of connections and decisions.

07 · Reflection and validation

Trust is an experience, not just a badge.

The concept brings verification, discovery and contextual guidance into one connected journey. The next step would be to validate whether users understand the verification process, feel comfortable sharing information and find AI-assisted recommendations genuinely useful.

What I would measure

Onboarding completion, verification drop-off, time to complete key tasks, perceived trust, recommendation relevance and user confidence during discovery. These are proposed evaluation measures, not measured project results.
